# FGA IG Saves Sorter

Sorts your saved Instagram folders into something you can use: recipes become recipe cards, AI finds become a to-test list, style saves become a swipe file, and inspiration becomes content ideas.

Install
- Claude (website or desktop app): Customize, then Skills, then + Add, then Upload skill, and drop in fga-ig-saves-sorter.skill as it is.
- ChatGPT: Plugins, then Skills, then Add, then Upload from your computer.
- Claude Code: unzip into ~/.claude/skills/ and restart Claude Code.

You also need a free Apify account connected, plus Notion or Google Drive. The skill checks both on its first run and gives you the steps for whatever's missing.

Then say "set up IG saves sorter". After setup, "sort my saves" runs it. It always tells you the cost and waits for your yes before spending anything.
