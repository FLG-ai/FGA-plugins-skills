# Style card for one reference reel

Write one card per reel in `refs/<id>.md`, then put its short version in the settings file under `references`. Good when someone could rebuild the look from the card alone.

## Layout
**<Creator>, "<what the reel is about>"** · <link> · <length>

**What they do**
- **Hook (first 3 seconds):** what is on screen from frame 1, where, and what changes.
- **Captions:** words at a time, size as a share of the frame width, position (for example "about 25% down, above the head"), font look (bold rounded sans, light serif italic, condensed caps), color, outline or shadow, and how key words differ.
- **Titles and big text:** font look, size, color, how it arrives (pops, types on, builds word by word), how long it stays.
- **Inserts:** screenshots, photos, clips or emoji. Size, position, border or none, when they appear.
- **Motion:** locked shot or cuts, zooms, pull-outs, framing (wide, chest up).
- **Sound:** music or none, effects on words.

**What to borrow:** two or three techniques, each named as a style (see `starter-set.md` for the names already in use, and coin a new name only when nothing fits).

## Translate before borrowing
- Black text on a yellow block becomes yellow or white text with a dark outline.
- Text a reference puts in Instagram's red zone (the top 250 px, below 1480 px, the outer 60 px, or the button column on the right) moves inside it.
- A thin serif over a busy background becomes the bold or outlined version.
