# Rules and who wins

## Hard limits (every lane, every person)
1. **Instagram's safe zone** on a 1080x1920 frame: nothing above y 250 (Reels header), nothing below y 1480 (username, caption, music line), nothing within 60 px of either side, nothing past x 940 between y 1050 and y 1480 (like, comment and share buttons). Windows and insets end by y 1360 so their small captions fit above 1480. The person can change these numbers only by measuring their own phone and giving new ones.
2. **Every spoken word is on screen as text**, start to finish: as a caption, or in a title or effect that shows those exact words (then they need no second caption). Under a window or cutaway the captions go smaller and sit below it. A word is never skipped.
3. **Real material only.** No invented facts, numbers, quotes or names. No logos they did not name. No other people's names, faces or messages from private screens: blur them or ask.
4. **Their footage stays on their computer.** Nothing is uploaded. The style board and the storyboard are served locally. Publishing one as an artifact uploads frames of their footage, so it needs a yes that says so. A paid generator (images, video, music, sound) needs a yes with the cost. Installs and downloads need a clear yes.
5. **Lip sync within 2 frames. Loudness about -14 LUFS, peaks under -1 dBFS.**
6. **One heavy job at a time** on a low-memory laptop.
7. **Never post, send or publish** for them.
8. **Never overwrite their original footage.** Outputs go next to the source or in the project folder.

## Who wins when rules disagree
1. The hard limits above.
2. The person's own `RK_HOME/style/RULES.md`, newest dated line first.
3. Their style: `STYLE.md` and `style.json` (and the mode section for the lane in use).
4. The method's mode files in `KIT/modes/`.
5. This skill's defaults.
The pack wins on taste. The kit and this skill win on mechanics. Hard limits win over both.

## RULES.md
Lives in the style folder. Start it from `BRIDGE/templates/RULES.template.md`. Read it at the start of every edit. When they correct something, ask "Want me to remember that?" and add a dated line with the reason. When two lines disagree, say so and follow the newer one. Never put their rules in the skill folder.

## Kit defaults people often change
The kit's defaults suit a generic creator. A style can override any of these, and a `RULES.md` line wins over the default:
| Kit default | Why someone changes it |
|---|---|
| Text and overlays pop in, with pop sounds | They prefer type that types on with soft clicks, or silent entrances |
| Giant numbers, giant captions, drum hits | They find them loud. The giant number pop and the scattered word pops are optional extras here: offered once, included only if the person wants them |
| Black-and-white beats and grade changes | They want one consistent grade on the footage |
| Lighter safe margins (top 220, bottom 1470, sides 35) | Instagram's real zone is larger. Hard limit 1 applies |
| Keep the last clean take of each line | The best take is often earlier. Compare every take |
| J-cuts that hold the outgoing picture 0.30 s past its words | That is where look-downs live. Set each join's lead by hand |
| One style pack, one look for every video | They want a different caption look per video with only one element fixed |
| Card and effect agents launched all at once | A laptop cannot take it. One video's builders at a time |
| Captions skipped where an effect carries the words | Fine when the effect shows the exact spoken words. A word that is on screen nowhere is never skipped |
